Design reference

Card effects卡片效果

Eleven card looks, each built to the brief. Move the pointer over the cards. Live pages use the frosted glass, spotlight, glowing dividers, tilt and halo; the rest are here to look at, and are switched on by adding a class (fx-…).十一种卡片效果，均按需求制作。请把鼠标移到卡片上试试。网站正式页面使用了毛玻璃、聚光灯、发光分割线、3D 倾斜和光晕；其余效果仅供查看，加上对应的类名（fx-…）即可启用。

01Frosted glass毛玻璃

20px background blur, white at 15%, a thin translucent white edge, and soft coloured blobs behind so the blur has something to show.背景模糊 20px，白色 15% 透明度，加一圈白色半透明细边；卡片背后放几团柔和彩色光斑，让模糊有东西可透。

Frosted card毛玻璃卡片

The colour behind it softens and glows through.背后的颜色被柔化，透出微光。

Another card另一张卡片

Same recipe: blur(20px), rgba(255,255,255,.15).同一配方：blur(20px)，rgba(255,255,255,.15)。

Edge light边缘微光

1px border at 35% white.1px 白色 35% 透明边框。

02Liquid glass液态玻璃

Thick glass: the rim bends and slightly magnifies what is behind it like a lens, the middle stays clear, and a thin highlight runs along the edge. The bending works in Chrome and Edge; Safari and Firefox show the same thick, lit-edge glass without the bending.厚玻璃质感：边缘像透镜一样把背后的内容轻微弯折、放大，中间保持清晰，边上有一道细高光。弯折效果在 Chrome 和 Edge 中可见；Safari 和 Firefox 显示同样的厚玻璃与边缘高光，但没有弯折。

Liquid glass液态玻璃

Look at the lettering and the chair at the edge.请看边缘处的文字和椅子。

03Holographic全息卡片

A rainbow sheen that changes angle and colour with the pointer, with a layer of fine glitter. Only for achievement badges and membership cards. The badges below use facts from the clinic's own material; the card is a sample, not an offer.卡片表面的彩虹色反光随鼠标位置改变角度和颜色，叠加细闪颗粒；只用在成就徽章和会员卡上。下面的徽章使用诊所自己资料中的事实；卡片仅为示例，不是真实优惠。

04Brushed metal拉丝金属

Dark grey metal with fine horizontal brushing, one diagonal highlight, and pressed-in (debossed) text.深灰金属底色，表面横向细密拉丝纹理，加一道斜向高光，文字为压印效果。

Pressed text压印文字

Engraved into the metal, not printed on it.像刻进金属里，而不是印在上面。

Brushed grain拉丝纹理

Fine lines run across the card.细纹横向贯穿卡片。

05Gradient border渐变描边

A 2px gradient border from the brand colour fading to transparent, on the one recommended card. The others keep a plain grey edge. Sample cards only: the clinic has no price list to show.推荐的那张卡加 2px 渐变边框，从品牌色过渡到透明；其他卡片保持普通灰边。仅为示例卡：诊所目前没有价目表可展示。

06Flowing border流光边框

A gradient ring that turns steadily around the card, one revolution every 8 seconds, with no flicker.卡片边框用一圈渐变色，沿边框匀速旋转，8 秒转一圈，不闪烁。

Flowing border流光边框

8s linear, endless.8 秒匀速，循环不停。

07Marquee border跑马灯描边

The border stays dark grey; one short bright run, a tenth of the border's length, travels around it in a loop.边框保持暗灰色，只有一小段亮光沿边框循环绕行，亮的那段约占边框长度的十分之一。

Marquee border跑马灯描边

10% lit, 90% dark.亮 10%，暗 90%。

Rounder corners更圆的角

The run follows the curve.亮光顺着圆角绕行。

08Spotlight聚光灯卡片

As the pointer moves over a card, a soft round light follows it, and the part of the border nearest the pointer brightens. Every card in the row has it, so the border glows on the neighbours too.鼠标在卡片上移动时，鼠标位置亮起一团柔和圆形光，边框靠近鼠标的部分也跟着变亮；一排卡片都有这个效果，相邻卡片的边框也会跟着发亮。

Card one卡片一

Card two卡片二

The light follows the pointer.光跟着鼠标走。

Card three卡片三

The border brightens near it.靠近鼠标的边框变亮。

Card four卡片四

Even before you enter.还没进入就开始发亮。

09Halo光晕

On a dark page, a large blurred pool of the brand colour sits behind the card and shows past its edges, so the card seems to float on light.深色页面上，卡片背后放一团品牌色大范围模糊光晕，从卡片边缘透出来，像卡片浮在光上。

Halo, bronze光晕（古铜）

The logo's own colour.取自标志本身的颜色。

Halo, steel blue光晕（钢蓝）

The site's second colour.网站的第二品牌色。

10Glowing divider发光分割线

Bright in the middle, fading to nothing at both ends, with a soft glow of the same colour, in place of a plain grey line. Used on the live pages in the news list, FAQ, focus list and footer.分割线中间亮、两端渐隐到透明，中间带一点同色柔光，代替普通灰线。正式页面的新闻列表、常见问题、关注领域和页脚已使用。

113D tilt3D 倾斜

The card tilts toward the pointer by up to 8° (the brief's limit is 10°), a highlight moves with the tilt, and it settles back smoothly when the pointer leaves.卡片随鼠标位置做 3D 倾斜，最大 8°（需求上限 10°），表面高光跟着倾斜方向移动，鼠标离开后平滑回正。

Highlight高光

It travels with the tilt.跟着倾斜移动。

Tilt + spotlight倾斜 + 聚光灯

They combine.两种效果可叠加。
