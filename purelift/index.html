As a health worker,
and as a patient作为医务工作者，
也作为一名患者

“Yes, this is exactly the kind of clinic I always wanted.”“没错，我一直想要的，就是这样一家诊所。”

As a health worker, and as a patient myself, I know what it feels like to be cared for well, and I often ask what we could do with a little more care.作为一名医务工作者，也作为一位患者，我体会过被细心照顾的感受，也常想，我们能把哪些事做得更细一点？

When I sat in the treatment chair as a client, what mattered to me was whether the person in front of me understood my concerns, and whether they explained the risks and the recovery clearly. Afterwards, if I had a question, I wanted to know who to contact. These ordinary details showed me where a clinic's time and budget should go.自己作为顾客坐在诊疗椅上时，我在意的是，眼前的人有没有听懂我的顾虑，有没有把治疗的风险和恢复过程讲清楚。治疗结束后，如果有疑问，我也希望知道该联系谁。这些看似平常的细节，让我明白该把诊所的时间和预算用在哪里。

In setting up PureLift Cosmetic, we kept the fit-out simple: a clean, comfortable space where people can talk with peace of mind. The budget we saved goes into professional training and refining our skills. During care, we also take a little more time for assessment, explanation and follow-up.筹备 PureLift Cosmetic 时，我们把装修做得简单一些，保留一个干净、舒适、让人能安心交谈的空间。省下来的预算，我们愿意花在专业培训和技术精进上。诊疗中，我们也愿意多留一点时间，做好评估、解释和随访。

Our philosophy我们的理念

7 things we stand by我们坚持的七件事

  1. 01

    Clear, transparent pricing价格透明，收费清晰

    We keep fit-out and running costs sensible and put resources into medical care and nursing. Before treatment we explain the cost and what it includes, so every expense is clear.合理控制装潢与运营开支，将资源投入医疗与护理。治疗前说明费用及包含内容，让每一笔支出都清楚明白。

  2. 02

    Medicine first, doctors and nurses together以医疗为本，医护协作

    Doctors and registered nurses each play their part, from assessment and treatment planning to aftercare and follow-up. Every step matters, and professional judgement is the foundation of care.由医生与注册护士各司其职，从评估、治疗方案到护理随访，重视每一个环节，让专业判断成为诊疗的基础。

  3. 03

    Your choice, no pressure尊重选择，不施压推销

    We never use anxiety to sell and never rush your decision. Whether to have treatment, and when to start, should rest on full understanding and your own free choice.不以焦虑促成消费，不催促您作出决定。是否接受治疗、何时开始，都应建立在充分了解与自主选择之上。

  4. 04

    Attentive care, careful listening用心服务，认真倾听

    A fair price still deserves careful service. We listen to what you need, answer your questions patiently, and respect your feelings and concerns.合理的价格，同样值得细致的服务。我们认真倾听您的需求，耐心解答疑问，尊重您的感受与顾虑。

  5. 05

    Careful treatment, safety first细致诊疗，重视安全

    From assessment before treatment and comfort during it, to aftercare and follow-up, we treat every detail seriously and are honest about each treatment's risks and limits.从治疗前评估、操作中的舒适度管理，到治疗后护理与随访，我们认真对待每一个细节，并如实说明治疗的风险与局限。

  6. 06

    A simple, clean space空间简洁，注重洁净

    Less ornament, more attention to cleanliness, privacy and function: a calm, orderly place for care.减少浮华装饰，重视清洁、隐私与实用功能，营造舒适、有序的诊疗环境。

  7. 07

    Fully informed, considered decisions充分知情，理性决定

    We explain the expected results, possible risks, alternatives and recovery clearly, and we respect a choice to wait or not to have treatment.清楚解释治疗的预期效果、潜在风险、替代方案及恢复过程，也尊重暂缓或不接受治疗的选择。

Philosophy

Our philosophy我们的理念

The heart of nursing, aesthetics with warmth以护理的初心，做有温度的医美

PureLift Cosmetic began with many conversations with colleagues while we were working as nurses in hospital.PureLift Cosmetic 的故事，始于我们在医院做护士时，与同事们的一次次聊天。

Many of them spent their days caring for patients, then went home to care for children and run a household; some were also supporting family overseas. When talk turned to skin care and aesthetic treatments, everyone wanted a little time to look after themselves, but because of the cost they usually put their children and family first and their own needs last.身边许多同事每天忙着照顾患者，下班后还要照顾孩子、操持家庭，有些还承担着赡养海外亲人的责任。聊起护肤和医美，大家也想花些时间照顾自己，但考虑到费用，往往还是先顾孩子、顾家人，把自己的需要放在后面。

Those conversations were where it all began. What if we let go of the “extras”, lavish fit-outs and heavy advertising, put the savings into better clinical skills and staff training, and passed them on in our prices? Isn't that the clinic I had always wanted to build as a health worker, and always hoped to find as a patient?这些交流正是一切的起点，如果能彻底舍弃华丽的装潢、过度的广告投放等这些“多余的东西”，并将由此节省下来的资源，投入到提升医疗技术、加强员工培训之中，再切实地反映在价格上。这不正是我作为医护人员一直渴望打造，同时也是作为患者一直向往的那家诊所吗？

That intention is where PureLift Cosmetic starts.这份初心，正是 PureLift Cosmetic 的起点。

  • Resources go into care and service把资源用在护理与服务上

    We work inside a medical centre and keep costs such as rent and fit-out under control, so more goes into professional care, safety and follow-up after treatment. This is how we keep our fees fair and transparent, so people can choose calmly, in step with their life and budget.我们选择在医疗中心内开展服务，通过控制场地、装修等运营成本，将更多资源投入专业护理、安全管理和治疗后的跟进。这也是我们努力保持收费合理、透明的方式，让有需要的人能够结合自己的生活与预算，从容作出选择。

  • We listen, and respect every decision认真倾听，尊重每个人的决定

    Years of nursing taught us to get to know a person first, then talk about the care they need. At PureLift Cosmetic we listen to your thoughts, assess your situation, and explain a treatment's possible benefits, risks, limits and costs clearly. Take your time; we respect your pace and your decision.多年的护理工作，让我们习惯先了解一个人，再讨论她需要怎样的照顾。在 PureLift Cosmetic，我们会认真听取您的想法，结合个人情况进行评估，清楚说明治疗的可能获益、风险、局限和费用。您可以慢慢考虑，我们尊重您的节奏与决定。

  • Your own features, in natural balance保留个人特点，重视自然与协调

    Everyone has their own features and their own hopes. Once we understand what you want, we aim to give suitable advice that balances a natural, harmonious look with long-term skin health. Every treatment should rest on good communication and realistic expectations.每个人都有自己的特点，也有不同的期待。我们希望在了解您的需求后，提供适合的建议，兼顾自然协调的美感与长期的皮肤健康。每一次治疗，都应建立在充分沟通和合理预期之上。

  • Caring for those who care for others也照顾那些一直在照顾他人的人

    We want to thank frontline workers, volunteers and people who support charitable causes, so PureLift offers them dedicated care pricing. Ask us about eligibility and fees.我们希望向一线工作者、志愿者和支持公益的人士表达一份感谢。因此，PureLift 为这些群体设有专属关怀价格，具体适用条件与收费可向我们咨询。

Clinical teaching

Clinical teaching: participants wanted临床教学参与者招募

From time to time, in line with our teaching plans, PureLift Cosmetic invites eligible adults to take part in clinical teaching. Before taking part, please note the following.PureLift Cosmetic 根据实际教学计划，不定期邀请符合条件的成年人参与临床教学。参与前，请了解以下事项：

  1. 01

    Eligibility参与条件

    Applicants must be 18 or over and have an individual clinical assessment. Whether treatment is suitable depends on your health, needs and risks; registering does not mean you will be selected or treated.申请者须年满 18 岁，并接受个别临床评估。是否适合治疗，将根据健康状况、个人需求及风险判断，报名不代表一定获选或接受治疗。

  2. 02

    How teaching works教学安排

    Before you take part, we explain what will be taught, the registration and experience of everyone involved, whether a learner will perform any part of the procedure and who supervises, and we obtain your explicit consent.参与前，我们会说明教学内容、参与人员的注册身份与相关经验，以及学习者是否参与操作、由谁监督，并取得您的明确同意。

  3. 03

    Treatment and fees治疗与费用

    We explain in advance the expected results, risks, alternatives, recovery and follow-up, and give you the fees and the cancellation and refund terms in writing.我们会提前说明治疗的预期效果、风险、替代选择、恢复及复诊安排，并提供书面费用与取消、退款条件。

  4. 04

    Photos and privacy照片与隐私

    Before any photo or video, we explain how it will be used, how it is stored and who can see it. Consent is asked separately for clinical records, teaching and public promotion. You can refuse public promotional photos, and fees are never tied to photo consent, reviews or social-media posts.拍照或录像前，会说明用途、保存方式及访问人员。临床记录、教学展示与公开宣传将分别征求同意。您可拒绝公开宣传照片，费用不与照片授权、评价或社交媒体分享挂钩。

  5. 05

    Voluntary participation and ongoing care自愿参与与后续照护

    You can ask questions, decline observers, or withdraw from the teaching arrangement before the procedure starts. If you have had treatment, we still provide aftercare advice, follow-up and management of any problems as clinically needed.您可以提问、拒绝观摩，或在操作开始前退出教学安排。如已接受治疗，我们仍会按临床需要提供护理指导、复诊及异常情况处理安排。

If you are interested, contact us to hear about current teaching plans and conditions.有意参与者，欢迎联系我们了解当前教学计划及具体条件。

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions常见问题

    Do I need a consultation before treatment?治疗前需要先咨询吗？

    Yes. We get to know you first: your goals, your health and your medicines. Then we explain the possible benefits, risks, limits and costs before you decide anything.需要。我们会先了解您：您的需求、健康状况和用药情况，并在您作出任何决定之前，说明可能的获益、风险、局限和费用。

    Who will look after me?由谁为我诊疗？

    Doctors and registered nurses, each in their own role, share your assessment, treatment and aftercare. There are no sales-driven “consultants”.
