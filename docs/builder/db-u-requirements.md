# Builder study: BPC Domestic Builder (Unlimited) requirements

Source of truth for any builder-study work in this repo. Checked against the Building and Plumbing Commission (BPC) website on 7 November 2026. Victoria only.

The BPC took over registration from the Victorian Building Authority (VBA) on 1 July 2025. Older VBA pages, forum posts and course marketing are often out of date; where they disagree with this file, this file wins until the BPC pages below say otherwise.

## Corrections to common source errors

These are the mistakes most likely to creep in. Each shows the wrong claim and the corrected one.

| Topic | ~~Commonly stated~~ | Correct (BPC, Nov 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Qualification | ~~CPC40120 Certificate IV in Building and Construction is the required qualification~~ | The Certificate IV is **not** a prescribed course for any class. It counts towards the broader assessment only, and applicants without the prescribed course may be asked to sit an additional exam. |
| Prescribed course | ~~Cert IV or Diploma~~ | Diploma of Building and Construction (Building) **CPC50210 or CPC50220**, or Holmesglen Bachelor of Construction Management and Economics, or Deakin Bachelor of Construction Management (Honours). |
| Assessment | ~~Computer-based plan exam plus face-to-face interview~~ | A **single online exam** replaced both, from January 2026, starting with DB-U. Interviews are removed. |
| Paperwork | ~~About 40 supporting documents~~ | About **7** documents. |
| Applying | ~~Hard-copy application to the VBA~~ | Online application through a BPC account. BPC says technical referee reports move online from June 2026, filed from the referee's own BPC account; its Domestic builder page still mentions signed forms, so confirm in the portal. |

## Requirements at a glance

| Requirement | What BPC asks for |
|---|---|
| Scope of the class | Carry out, manage and arrange all components of domestic building work on Class 1, 2, 4 and associated Class 10 buildings. |
| Optional conditions | Restricted to Class 1 and 10; or Class 1 and 10 plus Class 2 and 4 up to 3 storeys; or Class 2 and 4 only; or no conditions. Lets an applicant match the class to the experience they can show. |
| Qualification | Prescribed course above. |
| Experience: length | At least 3 years of practical experience. |
| Experience: recency | Gained in the last 7 years. |
| Experience: nature | Supervised by a registered building practitioner; paid (employee or contractor); across technical, supervision and contractual work. Owner-builder and training-course experience may be disregarded. Keep employment and contract records as evidence. |
| Technical referee | A Domestic Builder registered in the same class or higher who directly supervised the applicant. Form SD-TRR-052 (V9, March 2026). BPC's reforms page says reports move online in June 2026, submitted from the referee's own BPC account, but its Domestic builder page (updated 30 July 2026) still talks about signing the form. Confirm which applies in the portal. |
| Exam | Online, proctored (Janison-Proctortrack), randomised and frequently changed questions, designed with ACER. |
| Fit and proper | Declaration, criminal history check, External Administration Declaration where relevant. Partners in a partnership are assessed too. |
| Insurance | Domestic building insurance once knowledge and experience are accepted. |
| Company | A company can be registered in a class only if at least one director is registered in that class. |

## Experience the application must evidence

The BPC lists the activities a DB-U applicant must show they can do competently and to a professional standard. This list is also the best guide to the exam's scope, since no public syllabus or question bank exists.

1. Establish whether a building permit is needed.
2. Apply for and obtain a building permit.
3. Comply with a building permit.
4. Arrange certification of completed work when required.
5. Produce cost estimates and quotations.
6. Identify the need for, and establish, a major domestic building contract.
7. Plan and prepare for construction.
8. Arrange workers or subcontractors to carry out work safely and competently.
9. Develop, communicate, monitor and maintain site safety plans.
10. Ensure site safety inductions are done.
11. Supervise all construction work for compliance with the NCC, Australian Standards and local authority requirements.
12. Ensure work follows safety and environmental legislation.

## Exam rules that affect how to study

| Rule | Study implication |
|---|---|
| Question types: multiple choice, drag and drop, reorder, true/false, image select | Practise reordering sequences (permit → contract → construction → certification) and reading drawings, not only recall. |
| Reference material is viewed online inside the exam | Learn to navigate NCC Online quickly. |
| Australian Standards need the applicant's own active online subscription or purchase | Buy or confirm Standards access before booking, and know the login. |
| No printed notes, cheat sheets, phones, second screens, search engines or AI | Notes and apps are for preparation only. |
| Can be sat any time within the window the BPC gives; ID onboarding can take up to 3 days | Start onboarding as soon as the invitation arrives. |

## Key thresholds (Domestic Building Contracts Act 1995)

| Threshold | Rule |
|---|---|
| Over $10,000 | A major domestic building contract is required, and only a registered builder may enter it. |
| $16,000 or more | Domestic building insurance is required. |
| Building permit | The surveyor must be satisfied the builder is registered in the right class and insured before issuing it. |
