# Surface brief: language apps (Korean, Cantonese, English)

Scope: `/korean/`, `/yue/` and `/english/`, every tab. Visitor mode: **Operate**. `/mongolian/` keeps the old `style.css` look (owner's ruling, 3 November 2026). Product truth is in `PRODUCT.md`.

Chosen 3 November 2026 from three sketched directions (Metro line, OMR answer sheet, Noraebang lyric screen) and the category standard. The concept-seed roll was unavailable (no Impeccable engine in this environment).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The study track is a metro line and every card is a station sign. This refuses the category default of a grey stack of flashcards under a row of stat tiles.

OWN-WORLD: A platform-grey ground by day and a night-signboard charcoal ground at night. Cards are charcoal sign panels with white Hangul in heavy gothic and the hanja in signage yellow, with lighter subtitle lines beneath. Each app owns one line colour: Korean is Seoul Line 2 green, Cantonese is MTR Tsuen Wan red, English is Seoul Line 1 blue. That colour draws the rail, the you-are-here ring, the primary action, the active tab and the line badge (a roundel). Under each open sign runs a 4px line-colour rule.

STORY: You see where you are on the line, what is due and the word of the hour. You tap Learn or Review, read each card like a sign, and grade it with four plain buttons.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Home, 390 × 844):
- The header holds the roundel, the app name and the due pill.
- The line panel sits below it: the tier as the line name, then five tappable stations with the current unit ringed and the rail filled to where you are.
- The progress line, then Learn new (filled, line colour) and Review (outlined) follow.
- The word-of-the-hour sign comes next.

The signature interaction: tap any station to ride to that unit in Learn. On flip, the line-colour rule draws across the sign.

FORM: Seoul Metro / MTR wayfinding signage, first of the seven candidates. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
